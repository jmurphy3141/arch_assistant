# POV / PRFAQ quality rubric

## Dimension: visionary_press_release
5 = A vivid, account-specific future press release with a compelling headline, concrete customer stakes, and a credible OCI-enabled future state.
3 = A recognizable press release with some customer specificity, but the vision or narrative is generic in places.
1 = A generic executive summary or product description rather than a visionary press release.

## Dimension: oracle_and_customer_quotes
5 = Both a named Oracle leader and a named customer executive speak in distinct, credible voices that advance the story.
3 = Both sides are quoted, but names, roles, or voices are weak or formulaic.
1 = One or both quotes are absent, anonymous, fabricated as approved, or interchangeable marketing copy.

## Dimension: external_customer_faq
5 = The external FAQ directly answers the difficult commercial, technical, security, and adoption questions a real customer would ask.
3 = The FAQ is useful but misses important objections or gives shallow answers.
1 = The external FAQ is absent or consists of promotional talking points.

## Dimension: internal_oracle_faq
5 = The internal FAQ surfaces sharp discovery gaps, deal risks, owners, and evidence Oracle must obtain before making commitments.
3 = It contains reasonable internal questions but limited prioritization or actionability.
1 = The internal FAQ is absent or merely repeats customer-facing content.

## Dimension: grounded_persuasiveness
5 = The PRFAQ is persuasive because every specific claim is grounded in the engagement, with unknowns and proposed targets labelled honestly.
3 = The narrative is mostly grounded but contains generic filler or weak qualification of assumptions.
1 = It fabricates outcomes, ignores the customer context, or makes unsupported commitments.
