# Joint Execution Plan quality rubric

## Dimension: canonical_ten_section_structure
5 = All ten canonical sections are complete, ordered, mutually consistent, and usable without reconstruction by the SE.
3 = Most sections exist, but one or more are thin, renamed ambiguously, or disconnected.
1 = Major canonical sections are absent or the artifact is not recognizably a JEP.

## Dimension: execution_plan_craft
5 = The phased plan has sequenced activities, dates/windows, dependencies, owners, exit evidence, and a practical joint operating rhythm.
3 = Phases and activities exist but dependencies, evidence, or sequencing are underspecified.
1 = The execution plan is a generic checklist with no credible path to completion.

## Dimension: test_cases_and_success_criteria
5 = Test cases map cleanly to measurable success criteria, evidence collection, test conditions, and go/no-go decisions.
3 = Criteria are measurable but test method or evidence ownership is incomplete.
1 = Criteria are vague, fabricated, or disconnected from executable tests.

## Dimension: ownership_and_logistics
5 = Oracle and customer participants, responsibilities, approvals, access, locations, cadence, and logistics are explicit or honestly marked TBD.
3 = Basic ownership exists but important logistics or approvals remain implicit.
1 = Owners and logistics are absent, invented, or unusable.

## Dimension: customer_ready_execution_document
5 = The document is precise, concise, professional, and ready to run jointly with the named customer.
3 = It is usable after moderate SE editing.
1 = It reads like generated prose rather than an execution agreement.
