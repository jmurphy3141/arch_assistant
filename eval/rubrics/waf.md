# OCI Well-Architected review quality rubric

## Dimension: six_pillar_depth
5 = All six pillars are scored and supported by architecture-specific evidence, risks, and recommendations.
3 = All pillars appear but several are shallow or repetitive.
1 = Pillars are missing or reduced to generic best practices.

## Dimension: risk_prioritization
5 = Findings distinguish P1/P2/P3 severity, explain business impact, and identify the highest-leverage remediation sequence.
3 = Risks are sensible but prioritization or impact is inconsistent.
1 = Findings are unranked, alarmist, or disconnected from material risk.

## Dimension: oci_and_control_specificity
5 = Recommendations name correct OCI controls/services and use relevant CIS or compliance citations accurately.
3 = OCI guidance is mostly specific but citations or implementation detail are uneven.
1 = Advice is generic, incorrect, or padded with unsupported control references.

## Dimension: evidence_and_grounding
5 = Every finding distinguishes observed evidence, fixture facts, assumptions, and information still required.
3 = Most findings are grounded but some assumptions are presented too confidently.
1 = The review fabricates evidence or ignores the supplied architecture.

## Dimension: remediation_actionability
5 = Each important finding has a concrete owner-oriented action, validation evidence, and practical next step.
3 = Recommendations are useful but not consistently executable.
1 = Recommendations are vague aspirations without a path to closure.
